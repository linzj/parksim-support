Support

ParkSim is a top-down parking trainer: parallel parking, nose-in and reverse bays, with auto-park demonstrations that talk you through each step.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, iOS version and the scenario you were in.

Restoring the Full Version

Open the settings panel, tap Unlock the full version, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. The Full Version is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Driving on the left

ParkSim picks the side of the road from your region the first time it opens. Change it under Region & lessons › Drive on the left.

Better lesson voices

Lessons use the best voice installed on your device. For a more natural voice, download an Enhanced or Premium voice in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices.

No sound?

Game sounds follow the Ring/Silent switch. Spoken lessons play even in silent mode; turn them off under Spoken lessons if you prefer captions only.

ParkSim is a practice aid. It does not replace lessons with a qualified instructor, and test rules differ by country.

支援

ParkSim 是俯視角停車練習 App：路邊停車、車頭入庫、倒車入庫，並有自動停車示範，以語音逐步講解。

聯絡我們

回報問題或提出疑問，請在 GitHub 建立 issue，並附上裝置型號、iOS 版本與當時的場景。

回復購買完整版

打開設定面板，點解鎖完整版，再點回復購買。請使用購買時的 Apple 帳號。完整版可透過「家人共享」與家人共用。

退款

購買由 Apple 處理，請至 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請退款。

靠左行駛

首次開啟時，ParkSim 會依你的地區設定行駛方向。可在地區與教學 › 靠左行駛中更改。

更自然的教學語音

教學會使用裝置上最好的語音。可在 iOS「設定 › 輔助使用 › 朗讀與說話 › 聲音」下載「增強」或「高品質」語音。

沒有聲音？

遊戲音效會依照響鈴／靜音開關。語音教學在靜音模式下仍會播放；只想看字幕可關閉語音講解。

ParkSim 是練習輔助工具，不能取代合格教練的指導；各地考試規定不同。

靠左行驶

更自然的教学语音

教学会使用设备上最好的语音。可在 iOS“设置 › 辅助功能 › 朗读与讲话 › 声音”中下载“增强”或“优化”语音。

没有声音？

游戏音效跟随响铃／静音开关。语音教学在静音模式下仍会播放；只想看字幕可关闭语音讲解。

ParkSim 是练习辅助工具，不能替代合格教练的指导；各地考试规定不同。
